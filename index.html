Nile Valley

Mirage

Raise a desert camp into a great city. Every era brings new buildings, new vehicles and busier roads.

  • Drag roads, eight ways, to the driveways of the oasis, the palm grove and the Hearth; carry one on over the river and it becomes a bridge. Caravans haul goods, then drive back to park.
  • Fed homes grow when you bring them building materials, from tents to towers.
  • Homes without water or dates start a countdown. If it runs out, families leave: the home shrinks, or a tent is abandoned.
  • Sell goods at trade posts. Upkeep is paid every dawn; in debt the city stops growing, and deep in debt families start to leave. The city never ends: see how far it can go.
